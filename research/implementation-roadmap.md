# WindowBrief — Implementation roadmap

Status: draft aligned to WA-first master plan and editorial doctrine.

## First principles
- Australian homeowner problem before European product.
- WA-first and Australia-ready.
- Keep heritage character and local compliance separate from manufacturer claims.
- Expert guidance first; supply channel only after advice, with transparent disclosure.

## Required work packages
1. Confirm actual products, supply scope and WA support arrangements.
2. Build source-linked system cards and distinguish confirmed data from assumptions.
3. Define three linked layers: homeowner needs, WA-specific conditions, technologies/guides.
4. Prepare source-cleared images, technical SVG sections and educational diagrams.
5. Draft one full path: Cooler in Summer → Sun/Orientation in WA → Glazing Options → Project Brief.
6. Implement secure enquiry workflow and privacy/legal essentials.
7. Rebuild homepage and extend to 20–25 strong linked pages after testing.

## First information needed from owner
- List of products actually offered in WA, by manufacturer and system.
- Technical sheets, drawings and realistic configurations.
- Foils and powder-coat finish availability with codes.
- Photos with publication rights and location/project context.
- What the Fremantle partner really does (receiving, support, delivery, install, warranty).

## Don't publish yet
- Unsupported Australian compliance or BAL claims.
- Generic performance values not tied to exact configurations.
- Sensitive catalogue prices, supplier agreements or customer plans in public GitHub.
- Claims of local installation or service outside proven coverage.

## Gates
A. Scope and taxonomy signed off.
B. Product cards + image permissions available for sample journey.
C. Working first end-to-end content path and expert contact.
D. WA homepage + launch cluster approved.
E. Secure lead handling and legal/technical QA.
